How to Play Sudoku

Sudoku is a number puzzle where your goal is to fill the empty cells using numbers 1 to 9.

1. Understand the Rules

  • Each row must contain numbers 1 to 9 without repetition.
  • Each column must contain numbers 1 to 9 without repetition.
  • Each 3×3 box must contain numbers 1 to 9 without repetition.

2. How to Enter Numbers

  1. Click an empty cell on the Sudoku board.
  2. Choose a number from 1 to 9 using the number buttons or your keyboard.
  3. Repeat until you complete the puzzle.

3. Game Controls

  • Erase: Removes the number from the selected cell.
  • Hint: Shows the correct number for the selected cell.
  • Check: Checks whether your entered answers are correct.
  • New Game: Starts another puzzle.

4. How to Win

Fill every empty cell with the correct number while following all three Sudoku rules. When the entire board is correct, you win!
